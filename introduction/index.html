Chicken Adobo Recipe:

  • 2 lbs chicken thighs
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/2 cup white vinegar
  • 1 cup water
  • 6 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 teaspoon black peppercorns
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • Combine all in a pot, boil, then simmer covered for 30 minutes
  • Uncover and simmer 10 more minutes to thicken sauce
  • Serve over rice
